Privacy Policy

WizBudget — last updated August 27, 2026

WizBudget is a personal budgeting application operated by an individual (sole proprietor) for private, personal use. This policy explains what it collects, how that information is used, and who it is shared with.

Who operates this service

WizBudget is operated by Tom Wicorek, an individual in Washington State, United States. Questions about this policy can be sent to wizbudget@gmail.com.

What information is collected

  • Financial transaction data — dates, amounts, and merchant names from the operator's own bank and credit card accounts, obtained either from files the operator exports from their bank or through Plaid Inc., a financial data provider.
  • Mobile phone numbers — of the small number of people who have asked to receive budget summaries, currently the operator and one family member.
  • Budget and category settings entered by the operator.

No information is collected from members of the public. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

How the information is used

Transaction data is categorized and compared against budgets in order to produce a short daily summary. That summary is sent by SMS text message to the phone numbers described above. A typical message reads: "Aug 15: Food 31% ($1.6k left). Fuel 0% ($300 left)."

Who the information is shared with

  • Twilio Inc. — transmits the SMS messages. Twilio receives the recipient's phone number and the text of the message.
  • Plaid Inc. — supplies transaction data from the operator's own accounts, under credentials the operator provided directly to Plaid.

No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are used only to deliver the messages described in this policy. No data is shared with anyone else, for any purpose.

SMS messaging

  • Recipients receive approximately one message per day, plus occasional alerts when a budget threshold is crossed.
  • Consent is given directly by each recipient before any message is sent, and recipients are people known personally to the operator.
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

How the information is stored

Data is stored in a database file on the operator's own computer, not on a public server. Credentials for financial data providers are encrypted at rest. Data is retained for as long as the operator continues to use the application, and a recipient's phone number is deleted promptly on request or when they opt out.

Your choices

Any recipient may opt out at any time by replying STOP, or by contacting the address above, and may request that their phone number be deleted entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
