What this service is

WizBudget is a personal budgeting application operated by Tom Wicorek, an individual in Washington State, for private use by the operator and a small number of family members. It imports the operator's own bank and credit card transactions, sorts them into categories, compares them against budgets, and sends a short daily summary by SMS text message.
